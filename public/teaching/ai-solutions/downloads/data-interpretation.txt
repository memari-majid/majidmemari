5. READ THE FIGURE
Horizontal and vertical axis labels, units, and ranges:
Does the axis start at zero? If not, does the display exaggerate a difference?
Do the chart and table tell the same numerical story?
What uncertainty or important group is missing from the picture?

6. INTERPRET UNCERTAINTY
How well does the sample represent the people or tasks in the decision?
What plausible values or alternative assumptions could change the conclusion?
If an interval is supplied, what estimate and population does it describe?
Do not treat a sample result or an interval as proof of causation.
