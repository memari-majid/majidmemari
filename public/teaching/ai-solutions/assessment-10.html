Purpose

Demonstrate the implemented capstone and pitch its adoption or sale to the instructor/class and an available partner. Submit six to eight slides, the usable artifact, a concise controlled-deployment/user-test record, and handoff material. Present for seven minutes with approximately three minutes of Q&A. No custom software or public launch is required, but a mockup alone is insufficient. Use fictional, instructor-supplied, or public non-sensitive business information. Real outreach, public launch, and actual payments are optional and are not required for the course. Use available ready-made AI or supplied AI outputs with ordinary no-code business tools; no programming, paid subscription, custom application, or public software launch is required. The final capstone requires a usable artifact/service and controlled partner or classroom deployment with an actual user-test record. A manually operated solution is valid. Label facts, assumptions, case evidence, role-play, and AI-generated material accurately. Include a brief disclosure of any AI assistance and how you checked it. Use the instructor’s calendar for your live offering.

Evidence and AI use

Distinguish case facts, public sources, your assumptions, and AI-generated material. Identify the tool, its purpose, a representative prompt, and the checks and revisions you made. Simulated customer responses are not evidence of actual demand. You remain responsible for every claim and calculation.
