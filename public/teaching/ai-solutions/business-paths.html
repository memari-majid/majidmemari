Financial analyst · controller · business partner

Finance & accounting

Does the proposed improvement create enough value to justify its full cost?

Use AI to organize supplied financial information, explain a variance, or draft a management brief. Check the arithmetic, assumptions, and sources before a person acts.

A tangible capstone option

A monthly management-report kit: a verified variance table, a concise narrative, a sensitivity exhibit, and a human review checklist. Alternatively, design a scenario-planning service with one finished sample report.

Questions to ask of the numbers

  • Which costs recur, and which occur only once?
  • How does the recommendation change with volume, review time, or adoption?
  • Is the benefit released capacity, avoided expense, or an actual cash inflow?

Marketing analyst · brand manager · customer-experience lead

Marketing & customer experience

Does the message or experience improve a meaningful customer outcome?

Create reviewed campaign content, customer information, or a service experience. Separate a persuasive message from evidence of comprehension, conversion, and repeat use.

A tangible capstone option

A campaign product kit: a defined audience, three reviewed content pieces, a test plan, and a results brief using supplied numbers. Alternatively, create a customer-insight service with a finished sample and repeatable review process.

Questions to ask of the numbers

  • What is the conversion denominator, and are the audiences comparable?
  • Is the change a percentage-point difference or a relative percentage?
  • Does a higher response rate offset acquisition, fulfillment, and review costs?

General manager · consultant · business-unit leader

MBA, management & strategy

Which opportunity deserves limited attention, budget, and a bounded pilot?

Compare competing uses of AI, define a viable business model, and make an executive recommendation. Keep the scorecard, evidence, economics, and ownership connected.

A tangible capstone option

An executive decision pack: a prioritized opportunity portfolio, a tangible sample of the selected solution, a pilot charter, and a board-ready decision brief. Show what evidence would make you revise or stop.

Questions to ask of the numbers

  • Do the rankings depend on a fragile weight or assumption?
  • What is the opportunity cost of choosing one pilot over another?
  • Which measure would justify expansion, revision, or stopping?

Business analyst · product owner · information-systems manager

MIS & business analysis

Will the proposed information service produce dependable results within the business process?

Translate user needs into an understandable service or decision-support concept. Specify the information needed, human review, exception handling, vendor terms, and acceptance evidence.

A tangible capstone option

A decision-support service package: a source-to-claim evidence table, a reviewed sample report, a storyboard of the user experience, and an acceptance checklist. Deliver the usable report service or no-code experience in a controlled pilot, evaluate its use, and prepare the handoff. A production software application is optional.

Questions to ask of the numbers

  • What share of cases is correct, and which errors matter most?
  • How do review effort and exception volume affect total service cost?
  • What does a vendor claim measure, and which cases were excluded?

Operations analyst · service manager · process-improvement lead

Operations & service management

Does the complete process become more useful, reliable, and economical?

Improve a repetitive task, service handoff, or supplier decision. Compare the current process, a simpler improvement, and AI assistance using the same measures.

A tangible capstone option

A service improvement kit: an approved response set or operating guide, a human escalation map, a before-and-after workload table, and a measured pilot plan.

Questions to ask of the numbers

  • Does the mean hide long waits or a small group of difficult cases?
  • Does faster drafting still save time after review and correction?
  • Which quality or safety measure must remain above the pilot threshold?

Complete alternative application cases

Take a problem all the way to a useful solution.

These fictional case packets provide evidence, a buildable artifact, business numbers, a pilot, and an adoption or venture decision. Use one when your instructor selects it as the context for an existing assignment. They are alternatives, not extra assignments.

Finance

Build a Weekly Cash-Briefing Kit

Help the owner understand projected cash timing, the assumptions behind it, and the questions requiring attention. A clear numerical schedule and source-checked narrative are more useful than a confident AI summary that confuses cash with profit.

Marketing

Build and Sell a Reviewed Content Starter Kit

Small firms need accurate, usable campaign materials tied to a customer action, but generic AI copy can invent claims and fail to connect audience, channel, offer, and delivery.

MBA / Strategy

Design an AI-Assisted Proposal Service

Service providers lose time turning incomplete client requests into understandable, consistent proposals. Faster text generation alone does not solve missing requirements, unrealistic promises, or delivery capacity.

MIS

Build an Order-Exception Service Kit

Inconsistent triage and incomplete handoffs cause rework and inaccurate customer promises. A high overall routing score can hide costly missed exceptions.
