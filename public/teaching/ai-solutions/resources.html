Keep your work

Notes, checked preparation items, and study progress stay in this browser. They do not sync across devices. A JSON backup restores them elsewhere; a text export is easy to read in your project folder. Interactive practice has its own saved decisions and export button.

Earlier notes and privacy

This twelve-unit edition uses a new progress record. Earlier notes can be preserved below; old completion marks are not mapped to different lessons. Imported conflicting notes are kept as separate notes rather than silently replaced.

Use fictional or approved non-sensitive information. Clearing browser data removes local work. The optional course TA processes messages separately from these notes.
