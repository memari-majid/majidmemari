Milestone 5 · after Unit 12

Demonstration and handoff

A snapshot of the same project record.

Demonstrate the actual bounded task and defend the recommendation using the artifact, test/retest evidence, economics, and operating handoff. Provide a concise decision brief and presentation or an accessible equivalent using the offering’s announced arrangements.

This milestone’s internal rubric allocates 60% to the shared demonstration/handoff and 40% to individual mastery. In the full-course model, those contribute 15 and 10 percentage points respectively. The individual evidence comes from the checks near Milestones 3 and 5; do not grade it twice. Every learner explains a calculation, a decision, and a limitation. Self-paced completion does not imply instructor grading or a credential.
