Milestone 4 · after Unit 10

Viability and route to users

A snapshot of the same project record.

Update the project record with economics, capacity, offer, acquisition or internal-adoption route, a sales/approval conversation, and onboarding. One coherent internal or external context is sufficient. Do not create another venture alongside the existing capstone.

Separate observation from forecast, identify missing costs, and explain the decision under a less favorable scenario. A role-play can demonstrate the conversation; actual outreach, payment, or a positive commercial result is not required.
