Milestone 2 · after Unit 5

First usable solution

A snapshot of the same project record.

Provide the actual versioned artifact or supervised service kit, its operating workflow, three varied bench tests, a brief reviewer access/use check, and a meaningful revision with retest. A document-based service is sufficient when another person can complete the bounded task. A mock-up or demonstration deck alone is not.

Include the source card, input/output examples, review criteria, exception handoff, and manual fallback. Record assistance honestly. If another person has not attempted the task, show that requirement as pending rather than substituting an invented result.
