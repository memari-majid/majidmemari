Choose your practical capstone

Build something people can use.

Choose the form that fits your problem. Create a tangible artifact, test it, estimate its value, and define human review. You can use any path to explore a new business by identifying the customer, offer, price, delivery process, and first realistic test.

01

A digital product kit

Create a usable, reviewed content calendar, customer information pack, research brief, or set of business templates. Show who it helps and evaluate its accuracy and usefulness.

02

An AI-assisted service

Produce a finished sample report, marketing package, or customer-support response set. Document a repeatable process with clear review and escalation responsibilities.

03

A usable no-code experience

Build a form, slide-based experience, document workflow, or visual-tool prototype. Share it privately with a user or classroom reviewer, test a realistic task, and improve the experience from the feedback.

No programming is required. Implement a usable kit, repeatable service, or tested no-code experience. A private classroom pilot counts when a real partner is unavailable; verify every important claim.
