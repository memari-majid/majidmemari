What you need, what you will make, and how this works

Use an ordinary document, presentation, or approved AI tool. No paid subscription or external business partner is required. The supplied Cedar Desk case provides a complete classroom starting point.

Each unit follows Learn → Try → Build → Finish. Practice happens in the lesson. Build adds evidence to your project record. Marking a unit complete is a personal study marker, not a grade.

Your final work includes a usable artifact or repeatable service, an observed user task, revision and retest, a business offer or adoption case, and operating instructions. A mock-up alone is not enough.

Allow roughly 65–115 minutes per guided unit plus project work. The design estimate is 37–63 hours in total; actual timing and academic requirements depend on the offering. These estimates have not yet been validated with learners.

Use your instructor’s dates and submission arrangements. Self-study does not include grading or a credential. See the five milestones.
