Discuss the decision

Sixteen prompts. Better reasoning.

Practice stating a decision, supporting it with evidence, and improving a peer’s thinking. The reference semester format assigns 5 points per discussion; other uses may be ungraded.

Original contribution: 150–250 words, 3 points. Constructive reply: 75–100 words, 2 points. Follow your instructor’s calendar and discussion process. Independent learners may use these prompts as reflection exercises.

Module 00

Introduce Yourself and a Business Problem

Introduce your interests and your chosen fictional business. Describe one customer or operational problem worth solving, the person affected, and one outcome that would indicate improvement. Explain what you still need to learn before recommending AI. Write 150–250 words and respond to one classmate in 75–100 words with a specific question or improvement. Distinguish case facts, assumptions, and AI-generated suggestions; disclose any AI assistance. Use fictional or supplied case data. Use the instructor’s calendar for your live offering.
