Read with a purpose

A small reading library.

Start with the focused reading in each unit. No book purchase is required.

Original course explanations, cases, figures, and practice carry the sequence. External readings provide another perspective. Access and tool details can change; core tasks remain usable without a paid service.

Unit 1, 4, 11

The AI Fluency Framework — one-page guide ↗

Read the four competency definitions. Identify one decision about delegation, one instruction, one output check, and one responsibility in your task. The guide is one page; no account is required.

Unit 2

OpenStax — Descriptive Statistics introduction ↗

Read this short introduction to numerical and graphical descriptions. Explain why your choice of a summary should match the question you are investigating; the rest of the chapter is optional.

Unit 3, 7, 10, 12

Strategyzer — How to select the next best test ↗

Read the experiment-selection principles and the discovery/validation distinction. Name the uncertainty your next test addresses. The article’s suggested experiment cadence is not an added course requirement.

Unit 5, 9

IDEO.org Design Kit — Build & Run Prototypes ↗

Read the five short steps. Identify what your version helps you learn and how you will record feedback. For this capstone, the final prototype must also perform the bounded task; a mock-up alone is not the final deliverable.

Unit 6

OpenStax — Confidence Intervals introduction ↗

Read the opening explanation of point and interval estimates, stopping before the mathematical example. State why a sample estimate is uncertain. Derivations are optional.

Unit 8

Microsoft Learn — Understand cost drivers in generative AI ↗

Read this single cost-driver unit and identify an expense missing from your first estimate. Product examples illustrate the vendor’s perspective; buying or using those products is not required.

Optional books for deeper study

These serve different roles, not a required reading stack. Selection uses official descriptions, contents, and documented teaching use; it is not a full-text review of every book.

Disciplined Entrepreneurship · Bill Aulet
The expanded 2024 edition provides a framework for customers, value, pricing, acquisition, and economics. An optional entrepreneurship extension for learners commercializing their solution; it is not required for the core path.
Co-Intelligence · Ethan Mollick
An accessible companion for human judgment and working with AI. Reevaluate examples with current tools.
Testing Business Ideas · David J. Bland and Alexander Osterwalder
An optional experimentation guide for selecting tests; business language can be applied to other problem contexts.
Introductory Business Statistics 2e · OpenStax
A free quantitative companion. Read selected explanations of averages, proportions, sampling, and uncertainty; no full statistics sequence is required.
Instructor inspection candidate

Co-Existence: The Next Phase of AI is Mollick’s newer 2026 book, distinct from Co-Intelligence. Review a lawful copy before selecting chapters or replacing the established introductory reader.
