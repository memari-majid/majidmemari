Executive presentation outline

EXECUTIVE PRESENTATION OUTLINE

1. RECOMMENDATION
What should the business do, and what approval or next step are you requesting?

2. PROBLEM AND USER
Who is affected, and what evidence shows the problem matters?

3. CONCEPT IN USE
Show the implemented artifact or service and the controlled pilot result.
Use a storyboard to explain the experience, not as the only final deliverable.
Explain the customer's or employee's experience in plain language.

4. ALTERNATIVES AND EVIDENCE
What alternatives did you compare?
What feedback or test supports the choice?
What is still an assumption?

5. ECONOMICS AND RISKS
Show the key benefit and cost assumptions.
Explain one less favorable scenario.
Name the human review responsibility and the most important risk control.

6. NEXT STEP
Propose a bounded pilot or validation step with an owner, measure, and stop condition.

PREPARE FOR Q&A

What evidence would change your recommendation?
What will you say if asked for a fact you have not verified?
