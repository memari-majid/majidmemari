## Evidence categories

- **Supported concept**: the named passage directly supports the stated general principle.
- **Checked derivation**: arithmetic checked from explicitly fictional or hypothetical inputs; this is not an empirical result.
- **Original course method**: a practical teaching procedure or convention, with a related source where useful; do not claim the source prescribes that exact method.
- **Conditional model**: valid only under the stated assumptions; not a forecast established by classroom data.

### Source limitation discovered during review

Academic publication is not a guarantee that every sentence is correct. The later §8.2 regression/causality statement is scientifically overbroad and must remain outside the required scope. The course must continue distinguishing association, a bounded comparison, and causation. Narrow section assignments were chosen after reading the content, rather than treating a whole chapter as uniformly authoritative.

### Business bridge precision

Use **cash saving** for reduced cash spending. New cash receipts are a **cash benefit**, not necessarily a saving. This wording preserves the distinction taught by §9.4 and the original course's separation of valued time, recurring expense, and actual cash change.
