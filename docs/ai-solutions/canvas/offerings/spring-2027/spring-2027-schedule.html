Feedback, holidays, and the final exam

  • Use the two project-clinic weeks for actual user attempts, feedback, revision, and retesting. Keep an observation pending until it has occurred.
  • January 18 and February 15 are holidays. No live class or holiday response is expected. The listed Sunday deadlines remain; February 14 milestone feedback is targeted for February 22 because February 15 is excluded from working days.
  • Project exchanges are formative. The Friday initial-post dates are suggestions for allowing useful peer feedback, not extra graded deadlines. You can critique the supplied sample in the quiz project update instead.
  • For optional early exam feedback, submit an initial response by Sunday, May 2, 11:59 p.m.; the feedback target is May 4. Revise in the same assignment through the actual May 5, 11:59 p.m. deadline. May 2 is not an additional Canvas due date or a late-work boundary.
  • For this offering, feedback on on-time final exam submissions is targeted for May 10, replacing the general five-working-day exam target at term end. Native Canvas Grades and submission comments remain the feedback record.
