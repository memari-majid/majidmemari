Sent with conditions

A rescheduling request made at least 48 hours before your session may be considered, subject to available space and coordinator confirmation. Which later session are you requesting? No change has been confirmed.

The customer receives the policy conditions; no booking change is claimed.

The reply preserves the space and coordinator-confirmation conditions, and a person checks it before release. A sent reply is not a confirmed change.
