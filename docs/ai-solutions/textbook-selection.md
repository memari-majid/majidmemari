## Recommendation

Use **Bill Aulet’s 2024 expanded and updated _Disciplined Entrepreneurship_** as the business-development framework and **Ethan Mollick’s _Co-Intelligence_** as an accessible AI companion. Draw workshop methods from **_Testing Business Ideas_** and quantitative explanations from the free **OpenStax _Introductory Business Statistics 2e_**. Keep the implementation lessons, simulations, evaluation exercises, and partner project materials original and regularly updated.

These are recommendations based on fit, publisher material, and documented university use—not a claim that one book is universally best. Research covered official catalogs, tables of contents, samples, author resources, and public syllabi; it was not a full-text review of every book. Comparable courses illuminate parts of this course, but none of the verified examples exactly matches its complete partner-to-delivery-and-sales scope.

For students, preserve the existing free reading route. If a future offering adopts purchased books, use at most one business framework and one AI reader, with access established beforehand. Instructor references need not become student purchases. A business-partner project inside an existing organization can use sponsor commitment, adoption, and measurable operating value in place of startup revenue and investor funding.

## Priority shortlist

### 1. Best framework for the complete business journey

**Bill Aulet — _Disciplined Entrepreneurship: 24 Steps to a Successful Startup, Expanded & Updated_, second edition, Wiley, 2024.**

A structured entrepreneurship text covering customers, quantified value, pricing, customer acquisition, economics, assumptions, and product development. The updated edition integrates practical tools and worksheets. Its main teaching value here is a coherent sequence of decisions and evidence; AI implementation and quality evaluation still need original lessons. Adapt its startup language for internal partner projects. The publisher offers an evaluation-copy route and previews. [Wiley edition and contents](https://www.wiley.com/en-us/shop/general-introductory-business-management/disciplined-entrepreneurship-24-steps-to-a-successful-startup-expanded-updated-2nd-edition-p-9781394222520), [author’s edition guide and free sample chapters](https://www.d-eship.com/books/).

**Role:** Primary business framework; selected readings rather than a requirement to complete every startup step.

### 2. Best established introductory AI reader

**Ethan Mollick — _Co-Intelligence: Living and Working with AI_, Portfolio, 2024.**

An accessible trade book about working with AI, human judgment, and the strengths and limitations of these systems. It suits business learners without programming prerequisites. Its role is conceptual grounding and discussion, not platform instructions or a complete product-development curriculum. Update examples with current original activities. A publisher educator/exam-copy route is available; a full instructor package was not verified. [Publisher educator page](https://penguinrandomhousehighereducation.com/book/?isbn=9780593716717).

**Role:** AI companion, especially introductory modules. Its actual classroom use is documented below.

**Newer alternative:** Mollick’s _Co-Existence: The Next Phase of AI_ was published October 20, 2026. It addresses increasingly autonomous AI and human responsibility. It is a separate book, not a second edition of _Co-Intelligence_. Review it as a possible replacement, not another required purchase; public adoption evidence and teaching materials were not verified. The current course source inventory already recommends it as an optional instructor reference. [Publisher](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/813026/co-existence-by-ethan-mollick/).

### 3. Best visual experimentation handbook

**David J. Bland and Alexander Osterwalder — _Testing Business Ideas: A Field Guide for Rapid Experimentation_, Wiley, 2019.**

A practical illustrated handbook, with 43 experiment types organized by time, cost, and strength of evidence. Particularly useful when each partner supplies a different problem: teams identify an assumption, choose an experiment, set a decision threshold, collect evidence, and revise. The official site offers an 88-page sample, not the entire book free. [Official book site](https://www.strategyzer.com/library/testing-business-ideas-book), [Wiley edition](https://www.wiley.com/en-ca/testing-business-ideas-a-field-guide-for-rapid-experimentation-p-9781119551423).

**Role:** Instructor workshop reference and optional student companion. Pair it with original spreadsheet exercises on uncertainty, conversion, and cost; it is not a statistics textbook.

### 4. Best conventional introductory AI textbook to inspect

**Corinne Hoisington and Mark Ciampa — _Introduction to Artificial Intelligence: A Business Perspective_, first edition, Cengage, ©2026; released 2025.**

The strongest conventional introductory textbook candidate found for this brief. Its structure includes guided prompting, review questions, hands-on activities, and projects. MindTap is a separate access product. The instructor-resource area is gated; specific materials were not inspected. This is a broad AI survey, including robotics and computer vision, so select business-relevant chapters and omit coding-oriented activities. It does not supply the whole customer-discovery-to-sales capstone. [Publisher and access options](https://www.cengage.com/c/introduction-to-artificial-intelligence-a-business-perspective-1st-student-edition-1e-hoisington-ciampa/9798214024837), [contents and learning activities](https://www.cengage.com/c/mindtap-for-hoisington-ciampa-s-introduction-to-artificial-intelligence-a-business-perspective-1-term-instant-access-1e-hoisington-ciampa/9798214024332).

**Role:** Alternative AI foundation if conventional textbook structure is a priority. Inspect an instructor copy before replacing the shorter reader. Do not require both by default.

### 5. Best companion for business people creating technology solutions

**Thomas H. Davenport, Ian Barkin, and Chase Davenport — _All Hands on Tech: The AI-Powered Citizen Revolution_, first edition, Wiley, 2024.**

Uses citizen-development cases to connect domain expertise with automation, applications, analytics, and AI. Particularly relevant to employees creating solutions for partner organizations and to governance of that work. It is an organizational playbook rather than a step-by-step no-code lab manual. [Wiley](https://www.wiley-vch.de/en?isbn=9781394245901&option=com_eshop&view=product), [author’s book page](https://www.tomdavenport.com/book/all-hands-on-tech-the-ai-powered-citizen-revolution/).

**Role:** Selected instructor cases for implementation, organizational support, and responsible operation.

### 6. Best free quantitative companion

**Alexander Holmes, Barbara Illowsky, and Susan Dean — _Introductory Business Statistics 2e_, OpenStax, 2023.**

A conventional business-statistics textbook available free in web and PDF formats. Assign short explanations and examples on data, averages, proportions, and uncertainty. Students do not need to complete a full statistics sequence or its mathematical derivations. Continue using original course datasets and figures, with links to the relevant textbook sections. [OpenStax preface, scope, access, and license](https://openstax.org/books/introductory-business-statistics-2e/pages/preface).

**Role:** Free reference supporting the course’s tables, figures, experiments, and economic comparisons.

## Adoption and development decisions

1. Inspect evaluation copies of Aulet, Mollick, and the Cengage candidate. Compare reading accessibility, exercise quality, cross-business relevance, and preparation workload before choosing the student stack.
2. Review _Co-Existence_ against _Co-Intelligence_ for the next offering; choose one AI reader if a commercial reader is used. The present research favors _Co-Intelligence_ for its established classroom evidence, not because the newer book was found unsuitable.
3. Use _Testing Business Ideas_ and _Startup Tactics_ to inform workshops. Inspect actual educator resources before planning a class around gated slides or test banks.
4. Maintain original no-code implementation guides, evaluation tables, deployment activities, and handoff templates. Refresh platform instructions each offering; stable business books cannot keep those details current.
5. Keep source links and licensed access separate from course-owned content. Link to commercial texts and official samples; do not put book PDFs, publisher figures, or restricted instructor assets into the public course bundle. OpenStax currently specifies CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; direct reading links suit the portable course without assuming every later commercial use permits reproduction.
6. Once an offering’s reading choices are set, update its source inventory, syllabus, and public reading guide together. This research memo leaves the existing free path and optional references in place.
