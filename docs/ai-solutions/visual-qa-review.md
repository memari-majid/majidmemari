## Findings corrected and verified in source

1. **Authority is separate from answer correctness.** An acceptable draft may still require an owner decision. The workflow now records owner-review requirements separately from material errors. An unchecked reply that happens to be correct is explicitly labeled unchecked; it does not establish a performed source check or external action.
2. **Every advertised control has an action.** Unit 4's supplementary case table no longer presents inoperative inspection buttons. Its six-step workflow remains interactive; Unit 6 retains the twelve-case source inspector. Unrevealed policy placeholders are inert until revealed, avoiding dead keyboard stops with invisible focus.
3. **Discovery denominators do not invent overlap.** Six of eight reporting delay and three of eight willing to try are separate counts. Their overlap is not supplied, so the figure does not infer a three-of-six conversion rate. The test asserting that unsupported rate was removed.
4. **Feedback and tables remain accessible in structure.** The repair table has a labeled keyboard-focusable scroll region. Lesson-figure live feedback uses a persistent status node. Figures include textual explanations and matching tables; manual steps remain available and reduced-motion preferences disable automatic playback or animation.
5. **Time value is separate from cash.** The five-day queue example explicitly values added review time. It becomes cash expense only if additional time is paid, and it is distinct from the separate $60 monthly support assumption. Deferred requests remain counted rather than disappearing when intake is capped.
6. **Static explanations remain complete.** Compact figures retain worked fallback tables. Print styling exposes the complete fallback rather than printing a partially revealed interactive scene. The acquisition example names unique people and the day-30 observation point of a hypothetical campaign.
